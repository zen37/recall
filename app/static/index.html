About this screen

Semantic search over US openFDA food recalls, with an AI assistant and a personal watchlist. You're signed in via Databricks SSO; your watchlist is tied to your email.

🔎 Search recalls

Type a phrase like “listeria in deli meat”. It runs an instant semantic search and lists the closest-matching recalls. No AI — the same query always returns the same ranked list.

💬 Assistant

An AI helper that reads your wording and decides what to do:

• Ask a question (“any lettuce recalls?”) → it searches and answers in prose.
• Say “watch lettuce for me” → it adds that term to your watchlist.

Same-looking words, opposite intent — that's why this uses a model, not a plain search.

⭐ My watchlist

The terms you've asked to track. Each time you open or refresh the page, every term is searched live and its current matches are shown. Use “remove” to drop one.

Pull, not push. You see matches only when you open the app. Nothing runs in the background and nothing notifies you — this is not an alert system.
